Processing

Cleaning

Out-of-range readings and duplicate frames dropped, gaps marked (not interpolated), 5-sample moving median removes spikes.

Correction

PM2.5: C = 0.524·Craw − 0.0862·RH + 5.75. CO: ppm → mg/m³ with measured T and P (1 ppm ≈ 1.02 mg/m³ in Bengaluru).

AQI

CPCB sub-index interpolation; AQI = max sub-index. Real-time (1-min), hourly indicative (1-h) and CPCB-aligned (24-h PM2.5, 8-h CO).

Forecasting

ARIMA, Random Forest, XGBoost, LSTM and GRU against a persistence baseline, 12 h ahead from a 24-h window.

Control

Stage 0 / 1 / 2 at 0 / 50 / 100 % duty from current and forecast AQI, with a 10-min hysteresis before standby.

Purification

Reduction %, decay constant k, t₅₀, t₉₀ and CADReff = V(kon − koff).
